Privacy Policy

Package: com.mi.nooredeen  |  Effective Date: May 2025

1 Introduction

Welcome to Noor-e-Deen (نور الدين) — a free, fully offline Islamic companion app for Sunni Muslims. The app provides daily prayer times, an automated Azaan (call to prayer) alarm, a Qibla compass, and an Islamic calendar — all without requiring an internet connection.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses and how it is used. We are committed to full transparency: Noor-e-Deen does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with us or any third party.

🚫 No internet required 🚫 No user accounts 🚫 No analytics 🚫 No ads 🚫 No data sent to servers

2 Information We Collect

Noor-e-Deen does not collect any personally identifiable information. The only data stored is on your own device using Android's SharedPreferences (a private, sandboxed local storage) and includes:

  • Prayer times you set manually for each of the five daily prayers
  • App settings such as your chosen theme (light/dark) and display language
  • Whether the Azaan alarm feature is enabled or disabled

This data never leaves your device. It is never uploaded, synced, or shared in any form.

3 Location Data

The Qibla finder screen uses your device's location sensor to calculate the compass bearing toward the Kaaba in Mecca. Location is accessed only while that screen is open and the permission is granted.

  • Location coordinates are used in-memory for the Qibla calculation only.
  • Coordinates are never stored, logged, or transmitted.
  • If you deny location permission, all other app features continue to work normally.

4 Permissions We Use

The table below lists every Android permission declared by the app and explains exactly why it is needed.

Permission Why it is needed
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Re-schedule prayer alarms automatically after the device reboots, so you never miss a prayer time.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM Set precise prayer-time alarms on Android 12 — required by the OS for exact alarms.
USE_EXACT_ALARM Set precise prayer-time alarms on Android 13 and above — the newer OS-level replacement for SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Show the Azaan alert notification when a prayer time arrives (required on Android 13+).
WAKE_LOCK Keep the CPU awake long enough to play the full Azaan audio when the alarm fires, even if the screen is off.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE Run the Azaan audio playback as a foreground service so Android does not kill it mid-prayer.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK The required OS sub-type for foreground services that play audio.
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION Obtain a precise GPS location to calculate the exact Qibla direction toward Mecca. Used only inside the Qibla screen; never stored or transmitted.
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION Fallback location (network/cell) used if GPS is unavailable for the Qibla calculation.
REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS Asks the user to exempt the app from battery saver restrictions so prayer alarms fire reliably, even in low-power mode.
USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT Display the full-screen Azaan player on a locked or switched-off screen (required on Android 12+).
VIBRATE Vibrate the device when the Azaan notification fires to alert you even if the ringer is silent.

5 How We Use Your Information

All data stored by the app is used exclusively on your device to provide the app's core features:

  • Prayer alarms — saved prayer times are read by the alarm service to fire the Azaan at the correct moment.
  • Qibla compass — your momentary location is used to compute the bearing angle; the result is displayed on screen and immediately discarded.
  • App preferences — theme and language settings are applied at startup to personalise your experience.

No data is used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any commercial purpose.

6 Data Storage & Retention

All data is stored exclusively on your device using Android's SharedPreferences API. This is a private, app-sandboxed storage that other apps cannot access.

  • Data is retained as long as the app is installed.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all stored data.
  • You can also clear the data at any time via Settings → Apps → Noor-e-Deen → Storage → Clear Data.

No backups of your prayer time settings or preferences are made to any external server or cloud service.

7 Third-Party Services

Noor-e-Deen does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics libraries, advertising networks, crash-reporting tools, or cloud services. The app is entirely self-contained.

The only external interaction occurs when you download the app from Google Play, which is subject to Google's own Privacy Policy.

8 Data Security

Because no personal data is ever transmitted to any server, the primary security boundary is your device itself. We recommend:

  • Using a screen lock (PIN, pattern, or biometric) on your device.
  • Keeping your device's Android OS up to date to benefit from the latest security patches.

The app stores only non-sensitive preference data (prayer times and UI settings). Even if accessed by another app or a malicious actor with physical device access, this data poses no privacy risk.

9 Children's Privacy

Noor-e-Deen does not require user accounts, does not collect personal information, and does not contain in-app purchases or advertisements. The app is suitable for users of all ages, including children under 13.

Because no personal data is collected, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations do not impose any additional obligations on this app.
